# Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (ข้อ 1 - 60)

#### ข้อ 1
* **Topic**: Short and Long Vowels
* **Learning Objective**: Distinguish between short and long vowel sounds.
* **Difficulty**: Easy
* **Question**: Which word contains a short 'i' sound?
* ก. Kite
* ข. Stick
* ค. Light
* ง. Drive
* **Correct Answer**: ข
* **Explanation**: 'Stick' has a short 'i' sound /ɪ/. 'Kite', 'light', and 'drive' have the long 'i' sound /aɪ/.

#### ข้อ 2
* **Topic**: Vowel Teams
* **Learning Objective**: Identify vowel team patterns in Grade 6 vocabulary.
* **Difficulty**: Medium
* **Question**: Which word features the vowel team 'ea' pronounced as a short 'e' /ɛ/?
* ก. Beach
* ข. Leader
* ค. Heavy
* ง. Stream
* **Correct Answer**: ค
* **Explanation**: In 'heavy', 'ea' represents the short 'e' sound /ɛ/, unlike 'beach', 'leader', and 'stream' where 'ea' is long /iː/.
